A plotted development at Injambakkam

Icon Enclave is a residential plot layout by SSLF City & Housing, located at Injambakkam on Chennai's ECR–OMR belt. The layout offers 19 plots of 2,400 sq.ft and above, served by 20 ft and 23 ft internal roads.
